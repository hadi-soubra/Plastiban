A square icon illustration for a packaging manufacturer's website, one of a set
of six that must look like a single series.

STYLE
- Flat vector illustration. Clean, even geometric line work.
- No gradients, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no texture.
- Background: solid #12194a, filling the whole square edge to edge.
- Line work and primary shapes: #7f9bf0.
- At most one small accent element in #f7f5ef.
- Even medium stroke weight throughout.
- Subject centred, occupying about 60 percent of the frame, with generous and
  equal margin on all four sides.
- Simple enough to stay legible at 80 pixels wide.

ABSOLUTE RULES
- No text, no numbers, no letters, no labels, no watermarks, in any language.
- No people, no faces, no hands.
- No background pattern, grid, vignette or border.
- A single subject. No collage, no multiple scenes, no panels.
